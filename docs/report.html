Statements in the article its results don't support

  1. The described tuning doesn't produce the published tables.
    "Hyperparameters were optimized through a five-fold cross-validation process … GridSearchCV"

    Run as described, the search gives AEP results up to 16 MAPE points (18 CVRMSE points) away from Tables 8–10 (both on weekdays; up to 11.8 points in the harmonic means of the tuning table). The tables come from one fixed setting per model and dataset, hard-coded in the authors' explainability notebook and reused for all three input sets.

  2. The Conclusions name the wrong best model for AEP.
    "the GBM model, honed with only external factors, outperformed its counterparts … within the AEP dataset"

    Table 12 gives the lowest harmonic mean to CatBoost with all 15 inputs (48.23); GBM with external inputs has 50.82. (In Table 10, GBM leads on MAPE, RF on CVRMSE, CatBoost on NMAE.)

  3. The results text says external inputs gave the lowest errors.
    "the statistical values attained their nadir when external factors were considered"

    In Tables 5–10, all 15 inputs give the lowest value in all 45 dormitory cells and in 38 of 45 AEP cells; external inputs alone give the lowest value in only 7 AEP cells. By harmonic mean, all 15 inputs are best for every model except XGBoost on AEP.

  4. Figure 11 is described as the wrong model and data.
    "Fig 11 offers a clear interpretation of the GBM model's predictions for the test set"

    The figure reproduces exactly as CatBoost on the training hours.

  5. Figure 12's R² values are very probably in-sample.
    "Fig 12 indicates that the RF model achieved the most commendable R2 value"

    The article doesn't say how R² was computed. Refitted and scored on all hours, our RF reaches 0.93 (paper 0.964) with an almost identical scatter; on held-out weeks every model scores R² ≤ 0.05.

  6. The text on Figure 3 overstates the correlation.
    "Although a moderate correlation of about 0.6 was achieved"

    The diagram shows about 0.3–0.5 (ours 0.28–0.51).

  7. The holiday indicator isn't built as described.
    "we extracted holiday information from Time and Date … public holidays in both datasets"

    The authors' published AEP feature table marks weekends only (e.g. Ascension Day, 5 May 2016, is 0); we follow the table that produced the results.

  8. The Figure 13 text reverses WeekStatus.
    "the week status (indicating a weekend (0) or weekday (1))"

    Table 2 and the text on the inputs code weekday 0, weekend 1.

  9. The Table 14 paragraph has garbled numbers.
    "The respective values were 61 and 48.17 for CVRMSE, 87.31 and 92.46 for NMAE, and 48.66 and 55.78 for MAPE."

    The metric labels are shifted against Table 14 (48.17 is Bi-GRU's MAPE, 87.31 and 92.46 are CVRMSE), and "61" matches no value in the table (Bi-LSTM's MAPE is 37.61).

  10. Early stopping isn't mentioned.
    "we ran 100 epochs to balance adequate learning and prevent overfitting"

    The authors' notebook stops training early on a 10% validation split (patience 10). On AEP this matters: trained that way, our networks no longer produce exploding predictions (Table 14 below). The run also trains on 10% fewer windows, so stopping alone isn't isolated.
